Dear Councillors,

    I write in reference to the Peak Cluster project, to object to the proposals to build a carbon dioxide pipeline through Wirral.

    I object to this on the following grounds:

1) While the project will make certain products cheaper, this is not through any value being added but by avoiding higher taxes on building materials that produce high levels of CO2. Therefore, this is an artificial tax, and the value added by this project is also artificial. The same savings would be achieved by removing the Carbon Tax on these products.
2) Damaging and destroying nature in the name of Saving The Environment is absurd.
3) The danger to your local constituents' lives and the lives of their children is clear and immediate — if a leak should occur, which is not impossible, there is no limit to the number of people who will die of suffocation.
4) This area has a high degree of natural beauty. Destroying the view of the coastline with a large industrial plant is, in my opinion, criminal.
5) Even if the whole of the UK's Net Zero Goals were successfully reached, and even if they did not destroy this country's industrial sector, all the Carbon Dioxide saved from being emitted by this country would not equal a small fraction of that emitted by India and China, and those in government are deluded if they think that setting a good example will make other countries stop making money.
6) Nobody here or anywhere wants this on their doorstep.

In the event that you are asked by anyone from this project for your cooperation, I would request in the strongest possible terms that you refuse, and that you block this project with as much force as you are able to muster, on behalf of your constituents and their children, whose futures will be affected negatively if this goes ahead.

Yours sincerely,
